# Reports Backlog

Planned report cards for the `/reports` page. Tackle these one at a time.

## Phase 1 — Feeding ✅
Bar chart of feeds per day. Stat chips: avg nursing min/day, avg bottle ml/day.

## Phase 2 — Sleep
Bar chart of total sleep hours per day.
Additional stat: longest sleep stretch per day (tracks "sleeping through the night" milestone).

## Phase 3 — Diapers
Bar chart of total diapers per day with wet vs dirty breakdown annotation.

## Phase 4 — Wellness
Bar chart of spit-up + vomit events per day. Helps spot reflux patterns early.

## Stretch / Later

- **Longest sleep stretch per day** — show sleep consolidation progress alongside the sleep total bar
- **Breast-side balance** — left vs right breast minutes per day (helps ensure even supply)
- **Average inter-feed interval** — how many hours between feeds on average; shows whether intervals are stretching
- **Hourly feeding heatmap** — 24h grid showing which hours baby typically feeds (needs more complex bucketing)
